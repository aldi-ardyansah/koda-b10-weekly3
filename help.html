FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
HOW IT WORKS How does WargaReport get city issues fixed?
1. You spot an issue (pothole, water leak, dark street) and snap a photo. 2. WargaReport verifies the evidence and dispatches it directly to the responsible municipal agency (PAM JAYA, Bina Marga, Dishub, DLH, Satpol PP, Citata). 3. A public countdown starts. Neighbors can upvote the report to increase priority. 4. Field crews resolve the physical issue and post verification proof before closing.

STATUS MEANINGS What does each status mean?
Submitted Your report was submitted and received by our intake router.
Received Verified by WargaReport and queued for agency dispatch.
In review Agency coordinators are assessing field safety and equipment.
Assigned A field crew or technician is dispatched to the street.
Awaiting your info Inspector needs clarification or an updated photo.
Resolved Physical fix completed and inspection statement published.
Closed Case completed and archived.
